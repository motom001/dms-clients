Ablago is your own document archive for your home and small businesses. It runs on your own hardware, on a NAS, a small computer or with a hosting provider of your choice, and your documents stay with you. Scan letters or drop in PDFs: Ablago reads the text, recognises senders and deadlines, and files everything in folders such as contracts, insurance, vehicles, taxes and more.

This app brings the key numbers from your Ablago to Homey: how much new mail is waiting, what is to do and what is overdue. Turn on a lamp when mail has arrived, make an announcement, or send a message to your phone when something is due. As status indicator, the device tile shows the number you choose.

Every person sets up their own device with their own key of the level "nur Zähler" (counters only). The key can only read the numbers, never any content. The app runs on Homey Pro in your home network.
